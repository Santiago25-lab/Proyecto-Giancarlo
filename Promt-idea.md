UI/UX & MOTION DESIGN BRIEF: iNARIÑO VISUAL EXPERIENCE
Objective: Create a high-impact, premium visual prototype for iNariño. The focus is strictly on UI/UX design, smooth animations, and cinematic motion to present a high-end web proposal. No functional backend or database logic is required for this stage.

1. DESIGN AESTHETICS & BRANDING
Theme: "Dark Premium/Aggressive Tech".

Palette: Deep Black (#000000) base with Electric Red (#FF0000) accents, as seen in the brand's graphic identity.

Visual Philosophy: Combine Apple's minimalism with a gritty, urban entrepreneur energy.

Core Message: Highlight the slogan "No me compran por precio. Me compran por seguridad" with premium typography.

2. THE HERO EXPERIENCE (Motion Core)
Visual Layering: Create a 3D-depth effect using the provided assets.

Video Integration: Place the "iPhone/Accessory Explosion" video (client-provided) as the background or a side-hero element.

Founder Integration: Use the image of Giancarlo (transparent PNG) layered over the video.

Framer Motion / GSAP Task: Animate elements from the video (AirPods, iPhones) so they appear to fly behind and in front of the founder's image during the "soft explosion" sequence.

Entrance Animation: Implement a cinematic fade-in for the "iNariño" logo and the main security message.

3. INTERFACE MODULES (Visual Only)
Bento-Style Grid: A modern, interactive grid to showcase products like the AirPods and iPhone series.

Hover Effects: Smooth scaling and glow effects on product cards. When hovering, the accent color should shift to the Electric Red of the brand.

Scroll-Driven Motion: As the user scrolls, elements of the initial explosion should "settle" into their respective sections (Catalog, About, Contact).

Office/Team Showcase: A section designed to show the physical office and professional team environment to build trust.

4. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR DESIGN
Framework: Next.js with Tailwind CSS for styling.

Animation Engine: Framer Motion for UI transitions and GSAP for complex scroll-triggered sequences.

Image Optimization: Ensure the founder's portrait and the product shots remain crisp and high-resolution.

Responsiveness: The motion design must adapt fluidly from desktop to mobile without losing the "wow" factor.
